It moves with the car.

Braking, turning, every bump in the road — the platform leans, lifts and turns under the rider, from the game's own telemetry.

Six arms. One seat.

Six motors turn six cranks; six rods carry the platform. Between them, every way a car can move.

Made to fit.

The pedals and the wheel slide to the rider. The screen rides with the platform, always in front of the eyes.
